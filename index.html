Questions

Got questions? Good.

Is OranzAI a course?

It’s a community first. Programs and masterclasses are there when you want to go deeper.

Who is it for?

Video editors, animators and designers in India, and anyone curious about how creative work is changing with AI.

Do I need to know AI tools already?

No. We teach the craft first, then how to use AI inside it.

Is the masterclass free?

The community is free to join. Masterclasses have early-bird pricing for community members.

What language is it in?

Hinglish, the way most of India’s creators actually talk.

Can studios hire from OranzAI?

Yes. Reach out via the community and we’ll match you with creators who fit your brief.
